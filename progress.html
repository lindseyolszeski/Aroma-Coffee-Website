The Journey

Watching It Take Shape

We're early — a cart, a handful of followers, and a long-term vision. We want you to see it built in real time, one season at a time.

Some of what's on this page hasn't happened yet — a van, a future shop, a future kids' play space. Those are the vision, not a promise of exactly how they'll look. What's real right now is the cart, booked for weddings and events around Greenville, and a founder building this one honest step at a time. We think that's worth showing you too.

A Long-Term Vision

Phases of the Journey

Phase 1
In Progress

The Cart

Starting small — a coffee cart booked for weddings, graduation parties, bridal and baby showers, and events around Greenville. Low risk, real lessons, and a chance to build something true before taking on more. A full drink and food menu is still to come.

Phase 2
Upcoming

The Van

As the cart's story grows, the next step is a larger mobile van — more capacity for events, plus regular stops like farmers markets around Greenville.

Phase 3
Upcoming

The Building

Once the mobile side has a track record — real numbers, real stories, real proof of concept — the goal is a brick-and-mortar shop of our own in Greenville.

Phase 4
Upcoming

The Renovation

Building out the full shop experience — space to sit and stay, and room for the kids' play area we've wanted from the very beginning.

Phase 5
Upcoming

Impacting the Community

The long-term hope was never just coffee — it's using what's built to invest back into Greenville and beyond, including a possible second location in Spartanburg, SC.

A note on our menu: we're keeping things simple while we launch. Full drink and food menus are coming as we grow — follow along for updates.
